# We won but my score barely moved. Why?

W/L is now only **30%** of the score. Besides, if you beat a weak team, mu
barely rises anyway: OpenSkill rewards beating a strong team a lot and beating
a weak team only a little.

What really moves your score is how your in-game performance affects your
career average (**P_avg**). If you played above the match average, P_avg rises
and the 70% performance side of your score moves; if you had an ordinary game,
the score barely budges even though you won.

For the full math, see **"How is your score computed?"**.
